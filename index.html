DoggoJam

The host holds the sound. Guests play with a finger on the phone XY pad. Same local network, no app to install.

Join as Host

Laptop or tablet with speakers: drum machine, synth, effects, and guest touches.

Join as Controller

Guest phone: Dynamic XY touch pad. The page scrolls down. Pinch-zoom is off.

Liked this application? Share the link with others via QR 😉

Tone.js, Socket.io, and QRCode.js load from their official CDNs (MIT). The instrument itself is written from scratch.

AI use disclosure

The app was created using AI, but has no connection to AI-generated music.

The app's creator condemns the unethical use of AI, such as causing psychological and material harm to various communities and individuals, and the exploitation of intellectual property without respecting the will of their creators. The creator also acknowledges that the use of AI in the app's development may have indirectly contributed to the aforementioned problems.

The app's concept was created by humans, but some ideas for its elements were suggested by AI.

The following apps were used during development: Devin (including grok 4.7 and claud opus 5), Cursor (SWE-2 high), and the Google Search AI chatbot.
